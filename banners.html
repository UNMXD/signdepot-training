Banners: Finishing & Installing

How we finish a banner, start to finish: weld the hems, set the grommets, hang it right.

← Back to Production & Install

The full banner flow lives on this one page. Our welder is the Miller Weldmaster T-300. Our grommet press is a simple manual press.
