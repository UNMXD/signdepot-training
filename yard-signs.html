Yard Signs

Coroplast signs, start to finish: cut them down, stake them, and put them in the ground straight.

← Back to Production & Install

Yard signs are Coroplast (corrugated plastic). The channels inside are called flutes, and they run one way. Almost everything on this page comes back to the flutes: cutting is easier along them, and the stake prongs slide into them.

2. Put It in the Ground (H-Stakes)

  1. How to install yard signs without bending the stake (UZ Marketing)

    From a sign shop that installs these every day, two ways to do it. Soft ground: the sign can go on the stake first. Hard ground: drive the stake first, then slide the sign on. Either way the move is the same: foot on the middle bar, steady pressure, no stomping.
