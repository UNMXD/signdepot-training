Sales Spot Check: Are We Making Money on Materials?

Sign Depot USA  |  November 10, 2026  |  Prepared for the management meeting

← Back to Management

What we checked

Three recent estimates picked at random, and the three biggest recent orders, all in EVO. For each one we compared what the customer was charged (before tax) against the bill of materials cost EVO carries for the job. Nothing in EVO was changed; this was a read-only check.

What we found

1. Where real costs are entered, the jobs make money. The four fully costed jobs brought in $25,660.45 against $12,269.67 of materials and labor cost, leaving $13,390.78, about 52 percent.

2. Blind spot: Misc Items lines carry $0 cost. Two of the six checks used Misc Items lines with no material cost in EVO: the vinyl pouches estimate and the Forum CRE order ($1,200 charged, $0 cost recorded). On those jobs, EVO's profit number means nothing, because the real material cost was never entered.

3. Aluminum is costed as a full sheet on every line. On the Verus order, EVO charged a full sheet of aluminum ($480) to every sign line, whether the line made 2 signs or 4. That makes each 2-sign line look like a small loss on paper ($501.14 cost against $500 charged). The order as a whole still made about $5,132.

4. Hand-typed prices are common. The Salinas banner line, the McAllen ISD sign, the Primera Iglesia sign, and every line on the Verus order were priced by hand, over EVO's calculated price. In this sample, none of them lost money.

5. Free design labor on the Verus setup line. The print setup line shows $120 of layout labor discounted to $0, so the shop absorbs $77.44 of labor cost on that order.

6. One thing to check before printing. On the Verus order, line 17 is named DRY COOLER / LEFT but its description says DIRECTION-RIGHT, the same as line 18. The order is due November 13.

For the meeting

  • Should every Misc Items line require a cost before an estimate or order goes out?
  • Should aluminum be costed per sheet, or per square foot actually used?
  • Are hand-typed prices acceptable as long as the margin holds, or should an override need a reason noted?

Costs shown are EVO's stored costs as of November 10, 2026.
