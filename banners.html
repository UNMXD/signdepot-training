Banners: Finishing & Installing

Tips & Tricks

  • Two people on long banners. One feeds the welder, one guides the far end. Solo feeding is how hems wander.
  • Mark everything first. Mark all the grommet spots along the whole banner before the first press. Spacing mistakes are invisible on the table and obvious on the wall.
  • Keep it off the floor. Work on a clean table. Grit trapped under the vinyl scratches the print while you slide it around.
  • Roll it, never fold it. Finished banners store and travel rolled, print side out. A fold leaves a crease that never fully comes out.
