Notes vs. Chat

Notes tabs: the instructions

The standing directions each team follows: what to design, what to build, the deadline. Written once, and edited when the job changes. If it must be followed, it lives here.

The Chat: the conversation

The questions and answers between the people working the job right now. Read it to know what is happening with the job today.
