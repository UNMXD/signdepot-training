Handoffs: Design, Production, Install

How a job moves from one team to the next, and how everyone knows where it stands.

← Back to Sales

Jobs live in EVO, and so does the talk about them. The EVO order number is the key everywhere.

0 steps

Done. The job is handed off, and its whole story is on the record in EVO.

What Slack Is For

Slack is for quick pings only: a heads-up, a rush, a question that cannot wait, always with the order number and a link back to the order in EVO. Decisions, specs, files, and photos belong on the job in EVO.

The rule

If it is about a job, it goes in EVO. Slack just points at it.

That way, the next person who opens the job finds everything: what was asked, what was decided, and where it stands.

Why

Talk in a chat app scrolls away and gets lost. Talk on the job stays with the job, through every handoff, and long after the job is done.
