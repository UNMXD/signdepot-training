Summa S2 Class 160 T Plotter

This plotter cuts vinyl. Work the steps in order. Check the box when you finish a step.

← Back to Printing

The short version

This plotter cuts vinyl. Most jobs are a kiss cut: it cuts the vinyl, but not the paper under it. It can also cut all the way through (FlexCut). Biggest cut: 62 inches wide. The blade is turned by a motor. That is the tangential head, and it is why corners come out sharp.

Stuff this machine uses

  • Everyday blade: Tangential Knife 36°, part 390-534 (pack of 5). This is the vinyl blade.
  • Blade holder: part 395-322.
  • Thick or sandblast material: 60° blade, part 390-550.
  • Cutting strip: part 1NC40-67032. Change it when cuts stop coming out clean.
  • Cut-off razor blades: part 391-146.

Good to know

  • Widest cut: 62 inches (1580 mm). The roll can be up to 66 inches wide.
  • Knife pressure goes up to 600 grams on this head.
  • Does your machine have the OPOS camera? Some of these were built with it, some without. Look for the camera on the cutting head. If it is not there, use the normal OPOS marks.
  • On the computer: Summa Cutter Control sets up the machine. GoSign sends the cuts.
